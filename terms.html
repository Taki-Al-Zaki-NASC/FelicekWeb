5. Trust Deposit & Escrow

5.1 Freelancer Trust Deposit

Freelancer accounts require a one-time refundable $20 trust deposit. This deposit:

  • Is fully refundable after your first completed Job.
  • Demonstrates commitment and reduces spam/fraud.
  • Is held in a segregated trust fund account.
  • Can be withdrawn after completion of the first successful engagement.

If a Freelancer violates these Terms or engages in fraudulent activity, the deposit may be forfeited to compensate affected Clients.

5.2 Client Escrow

Clients must fund Escrow for each Job before work begins. Escrow funds:

  • Are held securely until Milestones are approved.
  • Are released to the Freelancer upon Client approval.
  • May be released in partial amounts for multi-Milestone Jobs.
  • Are returned to the Client if the Job is cancelled before work begins.

5.3 Escrow Disputes

If a dispute arises regarding Milestone completion, we may hold funds pending resolution. We may review communication logs, work samples, and other evidence to reach a fair decision. Our decision is final and binding.

6. Job Listings & Proposals

6.1 Posting Jobs

Clients may post Jobs with the following details:

  • Title: A clear, descriptive title.
  • Scope: A detailed description of the work required.
  • Budget: The total budget or budget range.
  • Milestones: Defined deliverables with associated amounts.
  • Skill Challenge: Optional timed task for applicants.
  • Skills: Required skills for the Job.

Jobs must be genuine, legal, and accurately described. Misleading or fraudulent listings will be removed, and the Client's account may be suspended.

6.2 Submitting Proposals

Freelancers may submit Proposals in response to Jobs. Proposals should include:

  • Bid Amount: The total amount the Freelancer is quoting.
  • Cover Note: A brief explanation of qualifications and approach.
  • Portfolio Links: Relevant work samples (recommended).

Proposals submitted using generic templates or irrelevant content may be flagged as spam and removed. AI spam filters are used to ensure quality.

6.3 Selection and Hiring

Clients may review Proposals, conduct interviews, and select the Freelancer they wish to hire. Once selected, the Client must fund Escrow before work begins. The Freelancer must accept the Job to formalize the engagement.

7. Skill Challenges

Skill Challenges are optional, timed tasks designed to assess Freelancer capabilities. They are created by Clients and completed by Freelancers within a specified time limit.

7.1 Challenge Format

  • Challenges are typically 4 minutes in duration.
  • Challenges test specific skills relevant to the Job.
  • Freelancers must complete the challenge before submitting a Proposal.
  • Challenge results are visible to the Client for review.

7.2 Challenge Integrity

Freelancers must complete Challenges independently. Assistance from others or use of AI tools to automate responses is strictly prohibited. Violations will result in immediate account suspension.

7.3 Client Review

Clients may view challenge submissions and use them to inform hiring decisions. Challenge results are not shared with other Freelancers. Clients should evaluate submissions fairly and without bias.

Fee transparency: The 1%–2% platform fee covers hosting, verification, escrow management, and support. There are no hidden fees for local payouts. International withdrawals may incur standard PayPal fees.
