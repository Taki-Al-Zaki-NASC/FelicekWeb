1. Introduction

Felicek ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our platform, website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

We value your trust and are dedicated to transparency. This policy describes your privacy rights and how data protection laws apply to you. By using our Services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Your privacy matters. We never sell your personal data. We only share information when necessary to provide our services, comply with law, or protect rights.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Services. The categories include:

2.1 Personal Identification Information

  • Account Data: Full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and profile details (bio, skills, hourly rate).
  • Verification Data: Government-issued ID (NID, passport), birth certificate, proof of address, business registration documents.
  • Payment Data: Payment method details (bKash, Nagad, PayPal, bank account), transaction history, and withdrawal records.
  • Communication Data: Messages, chat logs, emails, and support tickets.

2.2 Usage & Activity Data

  • Job Data: Job listings, proposals, bids, milestones, and skill challenge submissions.
  • Interaction Data: Clicks, views, search queries, and navigation patterns on the Platform.
  • Review Data: Ratings, feedback, and reviews submitted by Users.

2.3 Technical & Device Data

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device model, and unique identifiers.
  • Log Data: Timestamps, access times, error logs, and performance data.
  • Cookies: Small data files stored on your device to remember preferences and enhance functionality.

2.4 Data from Third Parties

  • Verification Providers: Identity validation services to confirm authenticity of documents.
  • Payment Processors: Transaction status, payment confirmations, and fraud detection.
  • Social Media: If you log in via social platforms, we may receive basic profile information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: Create and maintain your account, verify identity, and enforce role-based access.
  • Service Provision: Facilitate job postings, proposals, escrow, milestone releases, and skill challenges.
  • Communication: Send you important notifications (job updates, messages, payment confirmations, support responses).
  • Trust & Safety: Prevent fraud, spam, and abuse; enforce our Terms; and resolve disputes.
  • Analytics & Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience, optimize features, and develop new services.
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal requests.
  • Marketing: Send you promotional content (only with your consent; you may opt out at any time).
We never: sell your personal data, use it for unauthorized advertising, or share it with unaffiliated third parties without your explicit consent (except as required by law).

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases, depending on the context:

  • Contractual Necessity: To fulfill our obligations under the Terms of Service, including account registration, job facilitation, and payment processing.
  • Compliance with Law: To meet legal obligations, such as KYC/AML requirements, tax reporting, and law enforcement requests.
  • Legitimate Interests: To maintain platform security, prevent fraud, improve services, and send administrative communications. You can object to this processing.
  • Consent: For marketing communications, cookies, and certain data sharing. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We share your information only in limited circumstances:

5.1 With Other Users

  • Profile Information: Your public profile (name, skills, bio, reviews) is visible to other Users.
  • Job & Proposal Data: Clients see Freelancer proposals and vice versa.
  • Milestone & Delivery Data: Work products and deliverables are shared between the Client and Freelancer.

5.2 With Service Providers

  • Verification Providers: To validate identity documents (we share only what is necessary).
  • Payment Processors: To process transactions and manage escrow.
  • Hosting & Infrastructure: Cloud service providers that store and process data on our behalf.
  • Analytics & Support: Tools that help us improve the Platform and respond to inquiries.

5.3 With Legal & Regulatory Authorities

We may disclose data if required by law, subpoena, or governmental request. We also reserve the right to disclose data to protect our rights, users, or the public.

5.4 Business Transfers

If we undergo a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of the transaction. We will notify you of such changes.

6. International Data Transfers

Felicek operates from Bangladesh but may store and process data in multiple countries. Your data may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as standard contractual clauses and data protection agreements, to protect your data.

By using our Services, you consent to the transfer of your data to countries that may have different data protection laws than your own.

7. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL protocols.
  • Access Controls: Only authorized personnel have access to your data, and they are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Secure Storage: Sensitive information (such as ID documents) is encrypted at rest and stored in secure, access-controlled environments.
  • Regular Audits: We conduct security reviews and vulnerability assessments to identify and mitigate risks.
  • Incident Response: We have procedures in place to respond to security breaches and will notify affected users as required by law.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to using commercially reasonable measures.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

  • Account Data: Retained until you delete your account or request deletion.
  • Transaction Data: Retained for tax, auditing, and dispute resolution purposes for at least 7 years.
  • Verification Data: Retained for as long as your account is active, and then securely deleted after account closure (unless legal obligations require otherwise).
  • Logs & Analytics: Retained for up to 12 months for operational and security purposes.

After retention periods expire, we securely delete or anonymize your data.

9. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider in a structured format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously given consent for marketing or cookies.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@felicek.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may require identity verification before processing your request.

10. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for authentication, session management, and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your language, theme, and customization settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how you use the Platform (we use anonymized data).
  • Advertising Cookies: Not used unless you opt in for targeted ads (we do not run ads at this time).

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect Platform functionality.
