Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining our private beta and escrow security.

How does Felicek maintain flat 1–2% fees?
By utilizing direct KYC verification and streamlined smart escrow contracts, we eliminate unnecessary middleman administrative overheads and pass savings directly to users.
What is the additional 4% processing charge?
The 4% fee directly covers third-party credit card gateway charges, banking anti-fraud compliance, and instant cross-border currency payouts.
How are client escrow deposits protected?
Funds remain securely locked in segregated banking escrow accounts and are only released when project milestones are reviewed and approved by the client.
How does identity verification (KYC) stop proposal spam?
Because every account is tied to a verified passport or government ID, automated bots and copy-paste proposal spammers cannot operate on Felicek.
