Our Story & Ethos

Rebuilding Trust in
Global Work Economics

Felicek was founded on a simple premise: talent and hiring entities should connect directly without predatory 20% markups, unverified accounts, or fee extraction.

Our Core Mission

To deliver a secure, identity-verified peer-to-peer ecosystem where freelancers retain up to 98% of their earnings and clients pay zero arbitrary markups. We leverage automated escrow mechanics and real-identity credentials to eliminate the overhead that legacy platforms charge for.

Our Long-Term Vision

We envision a global talent marketplace powered by transparent verification and direct skill evaluation rather than bloated proposal systems. Our ultimate objective is to become the infrastructure standard for high-trust freelance engineering and digital design contracts.

Built on Three Core Pillars

How we fundamentally rethink traditional freelancing mechanics.

1. Radical Verification

Anonymous accounts, fraudulent profiles, and bot-generated proposals erode marketplace trust. Every user on Felicek undergoes real identity check prior to posting or applying.

2. Transparent Economics

Instead of extracting 20% to 25% commissions from every payout, Felicek charges a flat 1–2% platform rate plus standard 4% card processing settlement fees.

3. Guaranteed Protection

Milestones are fully funded upfront in secure escrow vault storage. Freelancers begin work knowing funds are committed, and clients release funds upon milestone approval.
