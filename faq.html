Knowledge Base

How can we help you?

Find answers regarding KYC onboarding, escrow vaults, fee calculations, and legal dispute protection.

What is Felicek and how is it different from Upwork or Fiverr?
Felicek is an identity-verified freelancing marketplace that eliminates 20–25% platform commissions. We charge a flat 1–2% platform rate, enforce real identity KYC for both clients and freelancers, and use direct milestone escrow accounts.
Who qualifies for Phase 1 Private Beta access?
Phase 1 access is strictly capped at 2,000 total seats (500 each for Freelancers, Individual Clients, Startups, and Agencies). Early applicants lock in guaranteed 1% lifetime platform fee rates.
Why is government ID verification mandatory before posting or bidding?
Mandatory identity verification prevents automated bot spam, fake profiles, and multi-accounting scams. It guarantees that clients only review proposals from real, vetted professionals.
How is my personal identity data stored and secured?
Felicek never stores raw identity documents on our primary servers. All document scanning and facial liveness checks are processed through Stripe Identity with end-to-end encryption.
How does Felicek maintain flat 1–2% platform fees?
By leveraging automated milestone contracts and identity-driven trust, we eliminate high administrative overhead. We don't take a quarter of your paycheck just to facilitate a contract.
What is the separate 4% payment processing fee?
The 4% fee covers credit card gateway charges, bank compliance, anti-fraud monitoring, and global currency transfers directly through our payment banking partners.
When are escrow funds released to the freelancer?
Clients deposit funds into the Escrow Vault at the start of a milestone. Once the freelancer submits the work and the client approves it, funds are unlocked and transferred immediately.
How does dispute resolution work if a client or freelancer disagrees?
Because both parties are identity-verified and milestones are documented via linked code or design submissions, disputes are resolved objectively by neutral arbitrators using submitted work records.
What happens if a client abandons an active contract?
If work has been submitted to escrow and the client becomes unresponsive for 14 calendar days, the system automatically releases the milestone funds to the freelancer.
